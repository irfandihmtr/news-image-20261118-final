# Headlines drawn on the poster — Wednesday 18 November 2026

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Wednesday
18 November 2026, and each was checked against at least two independent
sources before it was drawn. Claims made by a party to a story are
worded as claims on the poster.

## Lead — Bahrain and Kuwait intercept Iranian drone attacks overnight

Bahrain's air defences intercepted Iranian aerial attacks on Wednesday.
The Interior Ministry put out two threat alerts during the day, telling
residents to move to safe locations, with an all-clear after each.
Kuwait's army said its air defences were countering hostile drone
attacks early on Wednesday. Iran's army later said it had targeted US
drone and missile installations at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem base.
Background: this was the third Iranian drone-attack claim on Bahrain in
five days; on Tuesday US forces struck an IRGC missile launch site on
Qeshm Island after Tuesday's attack on the US radar at Sheikh Isa Air
Base.

Sources: Reuters wire published 2026-11-18T00:16:09Z; Al Jazeera
published 2026-11-18T03:56:10Z; Suryaa Now, 18 November 2026.

## 2 — Trump meets Marcos in Manila, then flies to Shenzhen

President Trump, in Manila since Tuesday, held a state visit and lunch
banquet with Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. at Malacanang
Palace on Wednesday, then flew to Shenzhen for the APEC summit. On
Thursday he meets Xi Jinping and then holds the first-ever trilateral
with Xi and Vladimir Putin, with nuclear arms control expected to top
the agenda. The Quad leaders met in Manila on Tuesday. The poster dates
the trilateral as Thursday, because that is when it happens.

Sources: AJP (Asia Journal Press), 18 November 2026; AmeriLedger,
2026-11-18T03:23:47Z; PBS NewsHour / Associated Press via TrueNews,
18 November 2026.

## 3 — Knesset elects Eisenkot ally Matan Kahana speaker, 66–52

Israel's Knesset elected Matan Kahana, an ally of opposition leader
Gadi Eisenkot, as speaker with 66 votes against 52 for Likud's Ofir
Katz, after a stormy seven-hour debate on Tuesday night. Kahana
replaces Amir Ohana, a Netanyahu ally. Eisenkot, tasked by President
Herzog with forming a government after the deadlocked 27 October
election, has until 6 December, with a possible 14-day extension;
Netanyahu stays on as caretaker prime minister.

Sources: Al Jazeera citing AFP and AP via RealNewsHub,
2026-11-18T01:43:02Z; Devdiscourse, updated 18-11-2026; Ekkow wire,
2026-11-18T01:18:54Z.

## 4 — UN warns of more cold deaths in Gaza as winter sets in

UN agencies warned that winter rains are worsening Gaza's shelter,
sanitation and food-security crisis. More than 1.7 million people
remain displaced and about 1.4 million face acute hunger; the WFP cut
hot meals in tent camps from one a day to one every two days. One baby
reportedly died of hypothermia, five people were killed when a
sandbank collapsed, and two children died in accidental tent fires.
Australia, Canada, Italy and Britain jointly urged Israel to remove
obstacles to humanitarian deliveries.

Sources: UN News, briefing in Geneva (Tuesday, carried 18 Nov);
AsiaOne, 18 November 2026; The Vaultz News, 17 November 2026.

## 5 — COP31 enters its decisive phase in Antalya

Negotiators at the COP31 climate talks in Antalya are working through
a first draft agreement of 144 items, many still disputed, as the
summit enters its final days before its scheduled close on Friday
20 November. The draft, published Monday by the Turkish and Australian
presidencies, calls for tripling adaptation finance to at least
$120 billion a year by 2035 and for accelerated action to phase out
fossil fuels; the Arab Group and Russia rejected it outright.

Sources: AsiaToday, 2026-11-17T12:20:42+07:00; COP31 Day 9 post,
2026-11-18T03:15:14Z; Pollar News, updated 17 November 2026.

## 6 — Microsoft calls Copilot "the new OS for work" at Ignite

At Microsoft's Ignite keynote in San Francisco, CEO Satya Nadella
called Copilot "the new OS for work". Word, Excel and PowerPoint are
now generally available inside Copilot; the rebuilt Copilot is
organised around Chat, Cowork, Code and Autopilot, with Copilot
Workspaces and autonomous Autopilot agents. Nadella opened the keynote
by apologising for the previous week's global Microsoft 365 outage.

Sources: The Official Microsoft Blog, 2026-11-17T18:36:03Z; YYSI brief
attributing Ars Technica, 18 November 2026; Oossa, 18 November 2026.
